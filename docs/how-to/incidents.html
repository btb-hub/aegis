01

Get the page

Incidents show a permanent label such as INC-01 in the web app and chat. The same label stays with the incident through handoff and resolution.

When you are primary on a team, a new incident for that team pages you in chat and appears under Incidents.

If a DM is missing, check Account → Paging messengers and confirm the bot can reach you. For eXpress, open a personal chat with the bot first. Paging connections are separate from sign-in. See paging identity.

Find and keep the right incident open

Use the status filter for All, Open, Acknowledged, or Resolved. On a phone it is a Status dropdown; wider screens show status buttons. A wide desktop shows the queue and detail side by side. On smaller screens, select an incident to open its detail, then use Back to incidents to return to the queue with the same status filter.

Selecting an incident adds its UUID to the page URL as incident_id; the readable INC-01 label is for people. A direct link opens that incident even when its status is outside the queue filter. After acknowledgement or resolution, the selected detail stays open even if the incident leaves the filtered queue. Changing the status filter clears the explicit selection and returns to the queue on smaller screens. If loading fails, use Retry in the affected panel.

02

Acknowledge, then investigate

Acknowledge

Click Acknowledge in chat or on the incident. The timeline records who and when. Use the chat button when you are on a phone or in a war room.

Read the same facts everyone else sees

Incident detail: linked alerts, Jira ticket (if connected), and the timeline. There are no hidden L3-only rows. L2 and L3 see the same history.

The Jira link uses the current Jira base URL for the owning team's workspace, including any context path. It follows that workspace's custom or inherited connector. If the slot is disabled or incomplete, no Jira link is shown; ask an administrator to check Integrations.

Resolve when it is fixed

Click Resolve. Prefer resolving in Aegis so MTTA/MTTR on Dashboard stay honest. Jira can stay your write-up surface.

03

Hand off to L3

On an L2-owned incident, click Hand off to L3, pick the configured L3 team, confirm Hand off. Aegis reassigns, pages that team’s on-call, and (when Jira is mapped) can update the ticket assignee.

No handoff button

The owning team has no outgoing path. Ask an admin to add one on Teams → that team → Escalation paths. You cannot invent a target from the incident screen.

04

Bounce back

When L3 cannot take it or needs L2 context, click Bounce to L2 (or bounce to L1 when that is the path). A note is required. The incident returns to the prior owner; the note is on the shared timeline.

Follow incidents in the on-call channel

Slack posts lifecycle messages to the team's configured channel. eXpress uses the global on-call group. Opening and escalation messages tag on-call users and include an acknowledge button while the incident is open. Personal pages continue. Message links open the selected incident, including resolved incidents.

A receipt means the acknowledgement request was queued. For a successful click in the configured eXpress on-call group, the shared acknowledged post confirms the change; there is no extra private success reply. Slack, personal eXpress chats, and unsuccessful or repeated clicks still receive private results. Repeated clicks report that the incident is already acknowledged or resolved. If your identity isn't linked, follow the linking guidance in the response.
